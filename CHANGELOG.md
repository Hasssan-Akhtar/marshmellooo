## 0.4.2

First public release.

- Ten flavours, each in Night and Day: Marshmallow, Bubblegum, Mint Chip, Peach Cobbler, Blueberry Pie, Matcha Latte, Lemon Drop, Cotton Candy, Sea Glass and Cherry Cola.
- Each flavour has its own text style (italic, bold and underline choices) and rainbow indent guides.
- Flavours panel in the activity bar: preview and apply flavours, switch Night/Day, pin a flavour to a project.
- Make your own flavour: pick a base, accent, sweetness and style, see it live across the editor, and keep up to three.
- Extras: per-flavour cursor and spacing (Personality), Follow the sun, Flavour of the day and a status bar picker.
